# Ordero rework — notes

## Reading of the brief
Mock rebuild of ordero.lt/en (Ordero Kids) as static HTML; later Ordero Business. Audience: parents first
(primary action "Get started" → app.ordero.lt/login), schools/caterers second ("Book a demo" → #contact).
Brand fixed: logo, colours mint #3ECFA3, coral #FF4D5E, amber #FFBE00, ink #232120. Type open.

## Inventory
1. Name/logo: "ordero", the o wears a mint brow, the r an amber smile, the d is coral: a face.
2. Three brand colours ↔ three parties: parents, caterers, schools.
3. Gedimino pr. 9, Vilnius (Go9) and Rådhuspladsen 16, København.
4. The cutoff: change or cancel any time before it.
5. The real menu in the app: 7-grain porridge with berries, fruit, unsweetened tea; mixed bean soup with
   fresh parsley and flaxseed oil, sour cream, lavash, slow-cooked turkey cubes with greens, fluffy
   buckwheat, green salad with cucumbers, radishes and peas, still water.
6. Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner chips; "Default" lunch 5,00 €.
7. Daily children's order report to the school; monthly report + waybills for caterers.
8. 0 paper forms, ~1 min to order a week, 100+ partners, 7-day human support.
9. iOS + Android apps.
10. Seven named partner schools/kindergartens (logos).
11. LT / EN / PL.
12. Three real testimonials: a mother, a teacher, a caterer.

## Argument
1. Hero: school & kindergarten meals ordered in one app, in minutes → app picture, Get started.
2. You see every dish before you order, and can change it until the cutoff → live menu card.
3. One order reaches three people at once (parent / caterer / school) → three coloured copies.
4. It is already working → 100+, 1 min, 0 forms, 7 days + partner logos.
5. All three parties say so → three real quotes.
6. Starting takes five minutes → the slip.
7. Start now → open "Add your child" dialog.
8. Schools & caterers: book a demo → coupon form.

## Idea
On this page, **the paper meal-order form Ordero replaced, made as a triplicate in the logo's three
colours (parent, caterer, school copies)** becomes **the page's stationery: card, slip, copies, coupon**.
Competitor test: another meal app has no mint/amber/coral copies and none of this menu.

Live thing: the day's menu card — switch Breakfast/Lunch/Dinner, order or skip, repeat weekly, and a
real countdown to the (sample) cutoff.
