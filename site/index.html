Every dish, before you order

Caterers put up the whole menu: breakfast, lunch, snacks and extras. You see each dish, set the portion and the allergies once, and order with one tap. Plans change, so you can edit right up to the cutoff.

Change or cancel until the cutoff at 10:00 the day before.

One order, three copies

A paper meal form went home, came back and was copied out twice. Ordero sends the same order to everyone at once, so the right meal reaches the right child.

The parent’s copy

Order for the week in about a minute, from your phone.

  • Personal. Meal type, portion and allergies, saved once and applied to every order.
  • Changeable. Edit or cancel right up to the cutoff.
  • Automatic. Set a default order for a period and Ordero repeats it every week.
  • Visible. The full menu, and a log of what your child actually ate.
